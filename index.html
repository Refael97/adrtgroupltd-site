Privacy policy

How ADRT GROUP LTD handles personal information.

Information we handle

We handle contact details such as names, phone numbers and email addresses that people give to us or to the businesses we work for, together with the content of their requests. When we operate phone and messaging services for a client business, we process this information on that business's behalf.

How it is used

The information is used to respond to requests, schedule and confirm appointments, and provide the service that was asked for. Calls handled by our AI assistants may be recorded and transcribed for scheduling and quality purposes.

Calls and text messages

Calls and text messages are sent only to people who contacted the business or agreed to be contacted. Message and data rates may apply. Anyone can reply STOP to a text message, or ask on a call not to be contacted again, and the request is honored.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share phone numbers or messaging consent with third parties for their own marketing. Information is shared only with the client business concerned and with service providers that help us deliver the service, or where the law requires it.

Your choices

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you.
